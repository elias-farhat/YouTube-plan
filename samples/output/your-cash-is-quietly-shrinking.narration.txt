Your Cash Is Quietly Shrinking

Hide ten thousand dollars in a drawer and it quietly loses almost half its value.

The thief is inflation. It works like compound interest in reverse.

At three percent a year, that cash slides to about fifty five hundred dollars in twenty years.

So make it earn more than inflation, like a savings account paying four percent

In twenty years, it grows to about twenty one thousand nine hundred dollars

After inflation, it still buys about twelve thousand dollars Double the drawer.

Real return is your interest rate minus inflation.
