title: The Sun You See Is 8 Minutes Old
channel: space
---
The Sun you see right now is eight minutes old.
> kinetic: The Sun you see | right now is | *8 minutes* old

Light is the fastest thing there is, about three hundred thousand kilometers every second.
> stat: label=Speed of light | from=0 | to=299792 | suffix= km/s | sub=Fast enough to circle the Earth seven times in one second

But the Sun is one hundred fifty million kilometers away, so the trip takes eight minutes and nineteen seconds.
> lightbeam: title=Sunlight's trip | from=Sun | to=Earth | time=8:19 | distance=149.6 million km

If the Sun vanished right now, we would keep seeing it shine for eight more minutes.
> orbit: vanish=1

Sunlight takes forty three minutes to reach Jupiter, and over four hours to reach Neptune.
> lightscale: Earth@8.3 min | Mars@12.7 min | Jupiter@43 min | Neptune@4.2 hours | title=Sunlight travel time | note=Spacing not to scale

And it has a lot to light up. A hundred and nine Earths fit across the Sun.
> sizes: count=109

So tonight, look up. You are seeing the past.
> kinetic: Look up. | You are seeing | the *past*.
