At 10 Meters Down, Red Disappears (Why the Ocean Loses Its Color)

DESCRIPTION
Why does everything turn blue underwater? Water absorbs light color by color. Red is almost gone within roughly 10 meters, orange and yellow go next, then green. Only blue light travels deep, and below about 1,000 meters there is no sunlight at all. That is why divers bring a torch: it puts the true colors back. Depths are approximate and depend on how clear the water is.
