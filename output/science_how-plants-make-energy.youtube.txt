A Tree Is Mostly Made of Air (How Plants Make Energy)

DESCRIPTION
How do plants make energy from sunlight? Leaves catch light in chloroplasts. Roots lift water. Tiny pores called stomata let in carbon dioxide. Light energy splits water, and that is where the oxygen we breathe comes from. The carbon from CO2 is then built into sugar, and the sugar becomes leaves and wood. That is why most of a tree's dry weight is carbon pulled out of the air.
