Why Divers Never Hold Their Breath (A Balloon at 30 m Is 1/4 Size)

DESCRIPTION
Every 10 meters of water adds about one atmosphere of pressure. At 30 meters the pressure is about 4 times the surface, so a balloon's air is squeezed to roughly a quarter of its volume. On the way up the same air expands again. That is why divers never hold their breath while ascending: the air in their lungs would expand too fast. Always breathe out slowly as you rise.
