Rome Should Have Died in 216 BC

In one afternoon, Rome lost fifty thousand soldiers.

Hannibal had fewer men. So he built a trap.

He let the Romans push his center back. Then he closed in from both sides.

Everyone waited for Rome to surrender.

Rome said no.

It armed slaves. It refused to pay ransom for its prisoners.

The general who lost? The Senate thanked him.

Hannibal sat in Italy for fifteen years. Rome never broke.

So Rome did the unthinkable. It sailed to Carthage.

Carthage burned for seventeen days.

Rome ended up owning the entire Mediterranean.

Rome lost the battles. It never lost the war.
