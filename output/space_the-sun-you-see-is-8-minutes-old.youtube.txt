The Sun You See Is 8 Minutes Old

DESCRIPTION
Sunlight takes 8 minutes and 19 seconds to reach Earth. If the Sun vanished right now, we would keep seeing it shine for 8 more minutes. Light needs about 43 minutes to reach Jupiter and over 4 hours to reach Neptune, and 109 Earths fit across the Sun.
