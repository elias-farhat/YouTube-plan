A Pulsar Talks to You (Pioneer 10's Secret Map)

DESCRIPTION
No narration, just a pulsar talking in pixels. In 1972, NASA bolted a plaque to the Pioneer 10 spacecraft with a map of where we are, made from 14 pulsars: dead stars that spin like lighthouses. Their spin slows at a known rate, so the plaque also tells the date.

The map in this video is a simplified illustration, not the exact plaque.
