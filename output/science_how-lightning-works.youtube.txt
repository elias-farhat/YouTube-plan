Lightning Is 5x Hotter Than the Sun. Here's How It Works

DESCRIPTION
How does lightning work? Ice crashes inside the storm cloud and splits electric charge. Air resists until it snaps, a hidden "stepped leader" feels its way down, and when it touches the ground the bright flash you actually see shoots UP, not down. It heats the air to about 30,000 degrees C, roughly five times the surface of the Sun, and the exploding air is thunder.

Bonus tip: count the seconds between flash and thunder. About 3 seconds is 1 km (5 seconds is 1 mile).
