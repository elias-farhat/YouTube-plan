How a Touchscreen Knows Where You Tap (It Feels Electricity)

DESCRIPTION
Most phone screens are capacitive. Under the glass is a grid of tiny transparent wires. Each crossing holds a small electric charge. Your finger conducts electricity, so it steals a little of that charge. The chip scans the grid, finds the crossing with the biggest change, and that is where you tapped. Insulating gloves block it, while many conductive things (even a banana) can work.
