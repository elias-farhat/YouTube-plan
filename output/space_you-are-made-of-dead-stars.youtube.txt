You Are Made of Dead Stars (Here's How)

DESCRIPTION
Almost every atom in your body was forged inside a star and thrown into space when it exploded. From the Big Bang to the supernova to you, in 40 seconds.

The Big Bang made almost only hydrogen and helium. Stars fused heavier elements, up to iron, and exploded as supernovae. That dust became new stars, planets, and us.
