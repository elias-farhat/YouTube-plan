You Are Made of Dead Stars

You are made of dead stars.

The Big Bang made almost nothing but hydrogen and helium.

Everything else was built inside stars.

They fuse hydrogen into helium, helium into carbon and oxygen, and heavier atoms all the way up to iron.

But fusing iron costs energy instead of making it. In under a second, the core collapses.

And the star explodes.

That blast scatters carbon, oxygen and iron across space.

Billions of years later, that dust formed new stars, planets, and you.

Apart from hydrogen, nearly every atom in your body was made in stars.
